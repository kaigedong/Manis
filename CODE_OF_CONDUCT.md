# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Manis community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn from the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, unwanted attention, or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Project maintainers
are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow them. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the reporter's privacy and safety.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
